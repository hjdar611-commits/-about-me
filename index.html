About Me

HJ

hiya! i’m hj.
a girl born on september twenty-eighth,
somewhere between being a little too quiet
and having a thousand thoughts at once.

i find comfort in music,
in stories that make me feel something,
in little moments that probably mean more to me
than they should.

i love creating things—
putting thoughts into words,
making little edits,
writing things that i’d probably never say out loud.

i’m still figuring myself out,
learning to accept the person i am
while slowly becoming the person i want to be.

sometimes i overthink.
sometimes i procrastinate.
sometimes i have absolutely no idea what i’m doing.
but i’m learning, and i think that counts for something.

i have dreams i haven’t figured out completely yet,
places i want to see,
things i want to try,
and a version of myself i haven’t met yet.

so for now,
this is me—
not finished,
not perfect,
just growing,
learning,
and becoming.
